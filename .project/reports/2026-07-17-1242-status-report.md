  sysml-codegen predicate and calc renderers still reject unary `+`.

  compiler rejects all equality.
- The agentic-mbse audit explicitly did not check the paired sysml-codegen branch, so its green
  **1484 passed / 1 skipped / 33 deselected** result is local certification, not cross-repo
  certification.
