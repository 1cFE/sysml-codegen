## The Point

`sysml-codegen` must preserve each modeled source occurrence through the executable boundary. One
externally supplied source becomes one public input, and one computed source becomes one producer
channel, across every calculation, constraint, and aggregation consumer. A legal self-binding may
be rejected, but it must never be reinterpreted as an outer reference or turned into independent
consumer-local study variables.
